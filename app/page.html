Uncalibrated model

Tune the station's HF amplifier the way Manual §4.5 describes: select the antenna, set the band, preset from Table 4-1, then peak forward power with TUNE and center the green TRI light with LOAD. The model's presets follow the manual's table, but its component values are placeholders until they are measured on the W3USR amplifier, so expect the feel to differ from the real one. This is a training aid; it does not authorize anyone to operate the station.

Band-change sprint

Press Start. You begin tuned on one band and must move to another, tuned, at 550 W forward or more.

Any unsafe action or protection trip disqualifies the run. Hold the tuned state, keyed, for one second to finish.
